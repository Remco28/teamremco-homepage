A little piece of the internet

Team Remco is part playground, part laboratory, an exhibit of our work, and just a tiny stake of virtual land on the internet. It's our presence there, a place to share a bit of ourselves.

Learning to build

Technology is making software development more accessible to everyone, and the ability to express yourself and your ideas has grown with it. Modern tools like agentic coding have let us build more instead of consume. Building is so much more satisfying than endlessly scrolling social media.

The projects on this site show the progress of our education in software. Right now one of us is doing the learning and bringing the kids along, and we hope they'll be contributing soon. The work goes in a loop: try something, learn from it, rebuild it, and take what we learned into something new. The goal is to keep learning and keep building.

Thinkspace

For twenty years we wanted a particular kind of tool. Plenty came close, but none was quite what we had in mind: fast, meant for pulling ideas together, with layers but without the bloat of a full image editor. It's as if the Snipping Tool, Excalidraw and Paint.NET had a baby.

Building it ourselves wasn't possible before. It isn't finished, but it works well, and we use it every day.

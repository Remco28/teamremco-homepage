# Voice: Team Remco site

> **Status:** Confirmed by Frank on 2026-10-26 (voice A: the story is Frank, features are neutral). Sample and jargon rule revised at Frank's request the same day: no meaningless numbers.
> **Covers:** the whole Team Remco site: every project's About page
> (`<project>.teamremco.org/about/`), the Projects page and the Support page.
> **Not:** the shared footer (fixed wording, see [about-pages.md](about-pages.md))
> and the apps' own screens, which keep each app's voice.
> **Based on:** Frank's Charmera voice transcript, the conversations planning the
> site (free to use, no ads, no guarantees), the Charmera app's own wording.

## Writer

Two voices on one page:

- **The story** is Frank, in the first person (*I*): someone who makes these
  apps for their own use and shares them. Their words come from a voice
  recording, lightly cleaned up.
- **The features and how-to** describe the app plainly, with no *I*: what it
  does and how to use it. Pages without a story (Projects, Support) use this
  plain voice too, with *I* only where Frank speaks personally, such as the
  "no guarantees" line.

## Audience

- **Who:** someone who tapped "about" in the app or on the Projects page.
- **Already knows:** usually the app exists; often owns the thing it is about
  (a Charmera, a crokinole board), but not always.
- **Came for:** why the app exists, and what it can do.
- **Reads it on/in:** a phone, often; a desktop browser otherwise.
- **Does next:** opens the app.
- **Least experienced reader we must not lose:** has never heard of the thing
  the app is about ("what is a Charmera?").

## Purpose

*Can the reader say why Frank made this app and what they can do with it, and
then open it?*

## Tone and stance

| Is | Is not |
|---|---|
| conversational | rambling |
| candid about flaws | apologetic |
| warm | cute |
| plain | salesy |
| proud of the details | boastful |

## Style rules

- Person: *I* in the story only; the feature text uses *you* for the reader and
  no *I*.
- Sentence length: mostly under 20 words. Contractions are fine.
- Jargon: no technical terms or numbers that mean nothing to the reader (canvas,
  EXIF, pixel sizes, megapixels). Labels the app itself shows, like PNG and JPG,
  are fine. Explain
  the app's subject once, early ("Kodak's tiny keychain camera").
- Spelling: American.
- Formatting: no exclamation marks or emoji in page text (text inside a
  screenshot, or alt text quoting it, is exempt). Headings in sentence case.
- Frank's story: cut filler and false starts, keep Frank's words, add no claims
  Frank did not make. Lines pulled out as quotes stay word for word, apart from
  filler.
